## ЧАСТЬ 3: BUYING JOURNEY

### Stage 1: Problem Recognition (0-2 weeks)
- Trigger event
- Initial research
- Mindset: "Есть ли решение вообще?"

### Stage 2: Information Gathering (1-3 weeks)
- Sources: G2, YouTube, blogs
- Key questions
- Mindset: "Какие варианты существуют?"

### Stage 3: Evaluation (1-2 weeks)
- Consideration set: 3-5 alternatives
- Decision criteria:

| Criterion | Weight (%) | Your solution | Competitor avg |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|----------------|
| Price | 25% | 8/10 | 6/10 |
| Features | 30% | 9/10 | 7/10 |

### Stage 4: Purchase Decision (3-7 days)
- Final objections
- Decision makers (B2C vs B2B)
- Mindset: "Покупаем или подумаем?"

### Stage 5: Post-Purchase (First 30 days)
- Buyer's remorse risks
- Prevention strategies

1. **"Слишком дорого"**

   - Response: ROI calculator
   - Messaging: "Pays for itself in 2 weeks"

2. **"Нужно время подумать"**
   - Real meaning: Not urgent
   - Response: Create urgency
   - Messaging: "Every day costs you $X"
